Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026  ·  Applies to the Spryzo mobile application, website and services

1.1 Introduction

Spryzo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Spryzo collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Spryzo mobile application, website and services.

By using Spryzo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1.2 Information We Collect

When you use Spryzo, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • GPS/location information
  • Date of birth, if requested by the app
  • Order history
  • Subscription information
  • Payment status and transaction information
  • Refund/cancellation information
  • Customer support communications
  • Information you voluntarily provide to us

1.3 Location

Spryzo may use GPS/location information to:

  • Check delivery availability
  • Identify the appropriate/nearest Spryzo service location
  • Process your order
  • Assign a delivery rider
  • Help the rider reach your address
  • Provide delivery tracking
  • Improve delivery operations

Location permissions can be controlled through your device settings.

1.4 Rider Tracking

Spryzo uses its own delivery riders. During an active delivery, Spryzo may use rider GPS information to show delivery progress and help manage the delivery. The rider may receive information necessary to complete your order, such as your name, delivery address and relevant order information.

1.5 Payment Information

Spryzo uses Razorpay for online payment processing. Depending on availability, payment methods may include:

  • UPI
  • Net banking
  • Wallets
  • Other Razorpay-supported methods
  • Cash on Delivery

Payment credentials may be processed directly by the relevant payment provider.

1.6 How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create your account
  • Process orders
  • Process subscriptions
  • Deliver products
  • Assign riders
  • Process payments
  • Process refunds
  • Provide customer support
  • Send order updates
  • Improve our services
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with applicable law

1.7 Sharing Information

We may share necessary information with:

  • Razorpay/payment providers

Spryzo does not intend to sell customer personal information.
