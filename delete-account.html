What happens to your data

  • Your account profile (name, mobile number, email, delivery addresses) is deleted.
  • Your order history and subscription records associated with the account are deleted.

Deletion requests are processed by our support team. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete. This process is described in our Privacy Policy (§1.10).
