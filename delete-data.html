Delete Your Data

Spryzo mobile app · Data deletion requests (without deleting your account)

You can request that some or all of your personal data be deleted while keeping your Spryzo account active — for example, removing saved delivery addresses or your order history.

How to request data deletion

Tap or click the button below. It opens your email app with a ready-made request — list the data you want deleted and press send.

📧 Send data deletion request email

Or write manually to spryzoapp@gmail.com with the subject "Spryzo Data Deletion Request", your registered mobile number, and which data you want removed.

What can be deleted without deleting your account

  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Order history
  • Subscription records
  • Support communications

What is retained

Records we are legally required to keep (for example, for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes) may be retained as permitted by law. See our Privacy Policy (§1.9–1.10).

If you want to delete your entire account instead, use our account deletion page.
